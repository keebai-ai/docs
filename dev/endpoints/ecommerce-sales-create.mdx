title: "POST /v1/ecommerce/sales"

description: "Record a sale or an internal consumption, and issue its receipt or invoice in the same call. Deducts stock and calculates commissions."

---

Creates a sale in Keebai. Stock is deducted from the branch, commissions are calculated, and, if you pass `dte`, the electronic tax document is issued right after. It is also the way to issue an invoice: a free line with a net price, a known customer RUT and a payment method are enough.

Everything that can fail — receiver data, payment, credit, branch, the document type, SKUs — is checked before the sale is created.

A `type: "internal"` sale records internal consumption instead: products taken out of stock for the business's own use, with no payments and no DTE.

## Endpoint

```
POST https://api.keebai.com/v1/ecommerce/sales
```

## Required scope

`ecommerce:sales:write`

Sending `dte` also needs `ecommerce:invoicing:issue`. Without it the request fails with `403 INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE` before the sale is created.

<Note>
  **The invoicing scope comes from your role.** Minting a token with `ecommerce:invoicing:issue` requires the permission to issue DTE in your Keebai role, unless you are the company owner or a superuser. See [scopes](/dev/scopes#scopes-are-independent-from-role-permissions).
</Note>

## Headers

| Header | Required | Value |
|---|---|---|
| `Authorization` | Yes | `Bearer kbai_pk_<token>` |
| `Content-Type` | Yes | `application/json` |
| `Idempotency-Key` | No | Your own unique key for this sale. Retrying with the same key does not create a second sale. |

## Body

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `type` | `string` | No | `sale` (default), a sale with payments, or `internal`, internal consumption: products only, no `payments`, no `dte`. |
| `branch_id` | `string` | No | Branch the sale happens in. Stock is deducted from it. Defaults to the company's only active branch; with several, the request fails with `BRANCH_REQUIRED`. |
| `items` | `object[]` | Yes | Line items: a catalog product or a free line. See below. |
| `customer` | `object` | No | `customer_id`, `name`, `email`, `phone`. |
| `seller` | `object` | No | `user_id` and `name`, both required when you send `seller`. |
| `payments` | `object[]` | No | Payments of the sale. See below. |
| `notes` | `string` | No | Free text. |
| `sold_at` | `string` | No | ISO 8601 date-time of the sale. |
| `currency` | `string` | No | Currency of the amounts. |
| `dte` | `object` | No | Issue the electronic tax document after creating the sale. See below. |

### Item

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `product_id` | `string` | No | `ObjectId` of the product. Send `product_id` or `sku` for a catalog product. Without both, the line is a free line. |
| `variant_id` | `string` | No | `ObjectId` of the variant. |
| `sku` | `string` | No | SKU or barcode. Resolves the product and variant without sending `product_id`. |
| `name` | `string` | Conditional | Line name. Defaults to the product's. Required on a free line. |
| `quantity` | `number` | Yes | Units. |
| `price` | `number` | Conditional | Unit price, VAT included. Send `price` or `net_price`, not both. Without either, a product line takes the product's, variant's, or price list's price. A free line needs one of them. |
| `net_price` | `number` | Conditional | Unit price before VAT, as you quote a business. The document shows exactly this net. |
| `discount_percentage` | `number` | No | Discount on the line, in percent. |
| `commissionee` | `object` | No | Who earns the commission of the line: `id` and `name`. |
| `exempt` | `boolean` | No | The line is exempt from VAT. |

### Payment

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `method` | `string` | Yes | Payment method. |
| `amount` | `number` | Conditional | Amount paid. With a single payment you can leave it out: the sale total is charged. With several payments each one needs its amount. |
| `reference` | `string` | No | External reference, such as a voucher number. |
| `installments` | `number` | No | Installments. |

### DTE

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `document` | `string` | Yes | `receipt` (boleta), `exempt_receipt` (boleta exenta), `invoice` (factura), or `exempt_invoice` (factura exenta). |
| `receiver` | `object` | Conditional | `rut`, `legal_name`, `activity`, `address`, `commune`, `email?`. Required for `invoice` and `exempt_invoice`. For a RUT the company invoiced before, `rut` alone is enough: the rest is filled in from the stored data, and any field you send overrides it. |
| `due_date` | `string` | No | `YYYY-MM-DD`. Due date of an invoice issued on credit, on a sale that is not fully paid. Defaults to today plus the company's credit days. Ignored when the sale is paid. |

Check which documents your company can issue today, and whether it allows credit invoices, with [`GET /v1/ecommerce/invoicing/types`](/dev/endpoints/ecommerce-invoicing-types).

## Response

### 201 Created

`sale` has the same shape as [`GET /v1/ecommerce/sales/{id}`](/dev/endpoints/ecommerce-sale-get). `dte` is present only when you sent `dte`.

```json

    "items": [
      {
        "kind": "product",
```

| Field | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `sale` | `object` | The created sale. |
| `dte.issued[]` | `object[]` | Documents issued: `sale_id`, `document`, `type` (SII code), `folio`, `document_id`, `status`, `net`, `exempt`, `tax`, `total`, and `due_date` for an invoice on credit. |
| `dte.failed[]` | `object[]` | Documents that could not be issued: `sale_id`, `reason` (the error code), `message?`. The reasons are listed in [`POST /v1/ecommerce/sales/{id}/dte`](/dev/endpoints/ecommerce-sale-dte-issue#failure-reasons). |

### 400 Bad Request

The body does not match the schema, a field the endpoint does not accept, or one of these. Nothing is created.

| `code` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SALE_ITEM_CUSTOM_INCOMPLETE` | A free line (no `product_id` and no `sku`) is missing `name`, or has neither `price` nor `net_price`. |
| `SALE_ITEM_INVALID` | A line has both `price` and `net_price`. |
| `PAYMENT_AMOUNT_REQUIRED` | Several payments, and one of them has no `amount`. |
| `PAYMENT_REQUIRED` | A `receipt` or `exempt_receipt` on a sale that is not fully paid. `pending` tells you what is missing when payments were sent. |
| `DUE_DATE_INVALID` | `due_date` is not `YYYY-MM-DD` or is before today. |
| `EXEMPT_DOCUMENT_REQUIRED` | Every line is exempt: use `exempt_invoice` or `exempt_receipt`. |
| `BRANCH_REQUIRED` | No `branch_id`, and the company has no active branch or more than one. `branches` lists them. |
| `INTERNAL_SALE_PAYMENTS_NOT_ALLOWED` | A `type: "internal"` sale carried `payments`. |
| `INTERNAL_SALE_DTE_NOT_ALLOWED` | A `type: "internal"` sale carried `dte`. |
| `INTERNAL_SALE_PRODUCTS_ONLY` | A `type: "internal"` sale carried a free line. Internal consumption only takes catalog products. |

### 401 Unauthorized

Missing, invalid, revoked, or expired token.

### 403 Forbidden

`INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE` — the token does not have `ecommerce:sales:write`, or it sent `dte` without `ecommerce:invoicing:issue`. Nothing is created.

### 422 Unprocessable Entity

| `code` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `RECEIVER_DATA_REQUIRED` | The invoice receiver is incomplete and the RUT has no stored data to fill it. `missing` lists the fields. |
| `CREDIT_NOT_ENABLED` | An `invoice` or `exempt_invoice` on a sale that is not fully paid, and the company has not enabled credit invoices. |
| `DOCUMENT_NOT_AVAILABLE` | The company cannot issue that document today. `available` lists the ones it can. |
| `SKU_NOT_FOUND` | No product has that `sku`. |
| `SKU_AMBIGUOUS` | The `sku` matches more than one product. |
| `INVALID_PAYMENT` | A payment is not valid for the company — for example a method it does not accept. |
| `STOCK_INSUFFICIENT_IN_BRANCH` | The branch does not have enough stock for one of the items. |

### 502 Bad Gateway

`ECOMMERCE_UPSTREAM_ERROR` — the ecommerce service could not answer.

## Operational notes

- **Send an `Idempotency-Key` from a point of sale.** A timeout does not tell you whether the sale was created. Retrying with the same key returns `201` with the original sale and creates nothing new; without it, a retry can double the sale and the stock deduction.
- **Errors are caught before anything is created.** Every `400` and `422` above leaves no sale and no stock movement behind.
- **A failed confirmation leaves nothing behind.** If the sale cannot be confirmed — for example `INVALID_PAYMENT` or `STOCK_INSUFFICIENT_IN_BRANCH` — the cart is discarded and the error is returned.
- **A failed DTE does not undo the sale.** The sale stays created and the failure comes back in `dte.failed`. Fix the cause and issue it later with [`POST /v1/ecommerce/sales/{id}/dte`](/dev/endpoints/ecommerce-sale-dte-issue).
- **A receipt needs a paid sale.** A receipt whose payments do not cover the sale fails with `PAYMENT_REQUIRED` before the sale is confirmed.
- **An invoice can go on credit.** When the company enables credit invoices in **Sales › Settings**, an `invoice` or `exempt_invoice` on a sale that is not fully paid is issued with a due date (`due_date`, or today plus the company's credit days). Otherwise it fails with `CREDIT_NOT_ENABLED` and nothing is created. Record the payment later from the Keebai portal or with the MCP tool `keebai_ecommerce_sale_payment_add`.
- **Net prices are exact.** With `net_price` the document shows exactly that net. A product line without its own price in the same sale keeps the catalog price it has at that moment.
- **`internal` never issues a DTE.** Internal consumption has no payments and no tax document; it only moves stock.
- **Lines are catalog products or free lines.** Send `product_id` (with `variant_id` when it has variants) or `sku` for a product; without `price`, `net_price` or `name`, the product's are used. A line with neither `product_id` nor `sku` is a free line, such as a service or a charge outside the catalog: it needs `name` and a price, and moves no stock.
- **Commissions are calculated on the sale.** `commissionee` on a line decides who earns it.
